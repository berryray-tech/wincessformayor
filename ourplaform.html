Housing & Affordability

A city that works for renters and buyers

Toronto's housing crisis is the #1 issue. We need to build faster, smarter, and more inclusively.

  • Unlock underused city-owned land for mixed-income housing
  • Expand rent-to-own programs for middle-class families
  • Streamline approvals to cut red tape, not safety
  • Protect existing rental stock with stronger enforcement

Transit & Mobility

Getting Toronto moving again

We need reliable, affordable transit that connects every corner of the city—not just downtown.

  • Accelerate the Eglinton Crosstown and Ontario Line
  • Integrate TTC with GO and regional transit seamlessly
  • Freeze fares for 2026 and expand the Fair Pass program
  • Build safe, connected bike lanes on main arteries

Community Safety

Safe streets, trusted policing

Safety means investing in communities, not just enforcement. We need a holistic approach.

  • Expand community crisis response teams (non-police)
  • Increase lighting and cameras in high-risk areas
  • Invest in youth programs & mental health supports
  • Rebuild trust between police and marginalized communities

Fiscal Responsibility

A city that spends wisely

We need to balance the books without cutting front-line services. Smart budgeting is non-negotiable.

  • Conduct a zero-based budget review every 2 years
  • Increase revenue through vacant home tax and commercial reform
  • Protect funding for libraries, parks, and community centres
  • Create a "Future Fund" for infrastructure reserves
