Wincess Dorville

Wincess Dorville for Mayor · Toronto

I was born and raised in Scarborough, and I've spent my life working alongside Torontonians in every ward. From my early days as a community organizer to my years in public service, I've seen firsthand what happens when City Hall forgets the people it serves.

I'm running for mayor because I believe Toronto can be a city where every neighbourhood thrives—not just the ones with the most lobbyists.

Born in Scarborough U of T · Public Policy Community Organizer, 12+ years Lives in Ward [Your Ward]

Rooted in Community

I cut my teeth organizing tenant rights groups in the 2010s, fought to save local parks from development, and helped launch one of the city's first community fridge networks. I don't just talk about Toronto—I've been showing up for it my whole life.

Built for the Work

Before this campaign, I spent 8 years in municipal policy—helping design affordable housing strategies and transit expansion plans. I know how City Hall works, and more importantly, I know how to make it work for you.

Driven by Purpose

In 2020, I watched my own neighbourhood struggle as small businesses closed and families fell behind on rent. That was the moment I realized: Toronto doesn't need more politicians. It needs leaders who are willing to fight.

Why I'm Running

I'm running because I believe in a Toronto where your postal code doesn't determine your opportunities. A Toronto where we build housing, not barriers. Where we trust each other, and where City Hall listens. That's the Toronto I know we can build—together.
